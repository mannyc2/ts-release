the reference contract. Their identified mechanisms and bounded product changes
are implemented, with explicit native/API retention decisions below. **The final

only to recorded inputs and the named profile. A retained compatibility/native
exception is a decision, not an unnamed open inventory. Finite unrun host/live
profiles and the missing historical dispatched candidate are evidence limits;
they do not imply additional compatibility code or broader authorization.
